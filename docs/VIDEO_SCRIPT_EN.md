## Recording rules

Use a fresh isolated sandbox order and preserve its run/commit/case evidence. Do not splice unrelated order IDs into one apparent transaction. If a wait is shortened, show a clear cut or time-lapse label; the final duration is presentation length, not a speed benchmark. No twelve-second approval target is required.

Keep the same buyer intent visible before and after repair. For dietary examples, use explicitly owner-confirmed metadata; do not suggest allergy safety. Show one refusal or UNKNOWN state clearly enough to read. Never replace an error with a fabricated success frame.

Capture desktop behavior and a brief real narrow-width view if it adds evidence. UI screenshots are not video footage. Add readable captions. Avoid unlicensed music, logos and third-party material; verify permission for visible PayPal/sponsor/source branding under the [submission rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules).

After editing, watch the public upload from a signed-out browser, confirm duration is below three minutes, verify all links and disclosure, and let an unfamiliar person explain the problem and result. The [official video requirement](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules) calls for actual functioning footage and a public YouTube link. A plan, local test recording or slide-only pitch does not establish the required completed demonstration.
